Research findings

What the current study sample shows.

The following figures are study-sample observations, not population-wide claims.

Young adult sample

The supplied young-adult guide reports a consenting study sample of 69 respondents aged 14–22.

Adult sample

The supplied adult analysis reports 30 consenting adult responses from the older participant / parent or guardian group.

Adult confidence

The adult report gives a mean self-rated confidence of 4.07/5 across the six scenario-confidence questions.

Cross-generational observation

The supplied guides describe a pattern in which young adults showed stronger domain-checking fluency but more vulnerability to social proof and FOMO-driven offers, while the adult sample showed greater deference to official/legal threats. Both groups were affected by high emotional urgency.

These observations are explicitly framed by the source material as tendencies within the project sample, not generalisations about all young adults or adults.
