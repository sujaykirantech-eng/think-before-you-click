The study

Scenario-based, cross-generational, family-aware.

Participants evaluate digital situations and describe what they notice, how they classify the message, what they would do next, and how confident they feel.

1

Consent & participant group

The questionnaire begins with voluntary consent and identifies the participant group for cross-generational analysis.

2

Digital context

Participants report common devices, transaction independence and how often they discuss suspicious messages or online safety with family.

3

Six message scenarios

Participants classify realistic messages, explain why they appear legitimate or suspicious, identify cues and choose an immediate action.

4

Psychological & family questions

The study also examines confidence, help-seeking, comfort admitting mistakes and support for two-person verification.
