Human psychology

The attack often starts with a decision.

The research focuses on the psychological cues that can make a digital request feel urgent, familiar, rewarding or authoritative.

Urgency

“Act now” reduces the time available for deliberate checking.

Authority bias

Official-looking identities can make people defer before independently verifying.

Social proof

People may infer safety because friends, classmates or other users supposedly accepted something.

Reciprocity

Unexpected help or a favor can create pressure to respond in kind.

Curiosity

Information gaps can create an urge to click simply to find out what is behind the message.

Sunk-cost pressure

Past time or money spent is not evidence that an unverified next step is safe.

THE 10-SECOND RESET

What am I feeling? Why do they want me to act now? Who benefits if I obey? What happens if I wait? Can I verify this outside the message?

Think. Verify. Act.
