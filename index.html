Why this matters

Cybersecurity is not only a technology problem.

Firewalls, authentication, encryption and security software are essential. But many digital threats also depend on a human decision: whether someone trusts a message, reacts to urgency, follows an authority figure, chases a reward, or pauses to verify.

Psychology helps us understand those moments. By studying the human side of cybersecurity, we can move beyond simply telling people to “be careful” and begin asking why people behave differently under digital pressure.

THE CENTRAL IDEA

A cyber incident may begin with technology — but the decision that changes its outcome is often human.

The research in one view

What does Think Before You Click investigate?

🧠 Human psychology

How urgency, trust, fear, reward, authority, curiosity and social influence can shape digital decisions.

🔐 Cybersecurity behaviour

How people assess suspicious or legitimate digital situations, what they would do next, and how confident they feel.

🌍 Cross-generational perspective

How decision-making patterns can differ across younger and older participant groups, including the role of family support.
