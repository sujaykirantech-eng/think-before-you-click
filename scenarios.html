Scenario-based research

Where the psychology appears.

The project deliberately uses situations rather than abstract definitions so participants can reason about decisions in context.

⚖️ Authority & fear

Legal, police, account or institutional messages can create pressure through perceived authority and consequences.

⚡ Urgency

Countdowns and short deadlines can compress decision time and reduce independent verification.

🎁 Reward & opportunity

Scholarships, internships, freelance offers and prizes can exploit excitement, FOMO and sunk-cost pressure.

🤝 Familiarity & social proof

A familiar logo, friend profile or “everyone is doing it” message can lower vigilance.

❤️ Emotional hijacking

Family emergencies and distress narratives can push people toward rapid payment or disclosure.

🔗 Verification friction

The safer alternative is often simple: leave the message, open the official service independently, and verify outside the original channel.
