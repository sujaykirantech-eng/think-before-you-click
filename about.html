About the project

People, psychology & safer digital decisions.

Think Before You Click is a student-led academic research initiative connecting cybersecurity awareness with human decision-making.

What the project asks

How do people respond when a digital message creates urgency, fear, authority, curiosity, reward or social pressure? The project uses scenario-based questions to examine those decisions.

Why psychology belongs here

A technically secure system can still be undermined by a rushed human decision. Understanding the psychological moment can help design better education and safer family habits.

Educational research site: realistic scenarios are synthetic models used for awareness and study purposes.
About the researcher

Sujay Kiran

Student researcher behind Think Before You Click, exploring the connection between cybersecurity behaviour, human psychology and digital decision-making.

The project focuses on understanding how people respond to urgency, authority, trust, reward, social influence and family support in everyday digital situations.
