Cross-generational

The design makes it possible to examine how different participant groups approach the same digital situations.

Scenario-based

Participants respond to situations rather than abstract cybersecurity definitions, allowing the study to focus on decision behaviour.

Family-aware

The questionnaire includes questions about family discussion, support and comfort asking for help after a possible mistake.
